Why I built it

MMarkA student Compass project at Western Reserve Academy

I built WRA STEM Kits because I want students to learn science by holding it in their hands instead of only reading about it in a textbook. I designed the kit to be affordable and reusable so that as many teachers and students as possible, anywhere in the world, can use it in their classrooms.

How it fits the NGSS

Students build and use a model. MS-LS1-2 asks students to “develop and use a model” of a cell. With the kit, they take that model apart, put it back together, and explain what each part does.NGSS practice: Developing and Using Models

Every part has a job. Each structure in a cell has a specific job; the cell membrane, for example, controls what goes in and out. Each piece of the kit is one of those structures, so students can study them one at a time.NGSS core idea: LS1.A Structure and Function

The parts work as one. A cell stays alive because its parts work together. When students fit every piece back into its slot, they see how the parts make up one cell.NGSS crosscutting concept: Structure and Function
