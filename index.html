Plant Cell Kit · Grades 6–7 · Designed to align with NGSS MS-LS1-2

12 printed pieces. Every one comes out.

Students lift the nucleus, chloroplast and mitochondria out of the cell, learn what each part does, then press it back into place.

Every piece is 3D printed, which keeps a classroom set affordable.

Why I built it

MTMark TangStudent, Western Reserve Academy · Hudson, Ohio

Cells are too small to see, and a flat diagram makes it hard to picture how the parts fit together. I wanted students to hold a plant cell in their hands, take it apart, and put it back together.

So I modeled the cell in CAD and 3D printed every piece. Then I built a worksheet generator, so a teacher can print a fresh worksheet and answer key that match the kit in one click.
