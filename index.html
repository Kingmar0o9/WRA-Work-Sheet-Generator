Plant Cell Kit · Grades 6–8 · Designed to align with Next Generation Science Standards* MS-LS1-2

12 custom-designed, 3D printed pieces.

Each organelle sits in its own custom-designed slot. Students lift a part out, name it, explain its job, and set it back: a working model of the cell as a system of parts.

Reusable by you, and by the classes and students to come.

Why I built it

MMarkA student Compass project at Western Reserve Academy

I built WRA STEM Kits because I want to help students learn science by holding it in their hands, not just reading about it in a textbook. I designed the kit to be affordable and reusable, so that as many teachers and students as possible, anywhere in the world, can use it in their classrooms.

How it fits the NGSS

Students build and use a model. MS-LS1-2 asks students to “develop and use a model” of a cell. The kit is that model: they take it apart, put it back together, and explain what each part does.NGSS practice: Developing and Using Models

Every part has a job. Each structure in a cell does something specific, and the cell membrane controls what goes in and out. Each piece of the kit is one of those structures.NGSS core idea: LS1.A Structure and Function

The parts work as one. A cell works because its parts work together. Fitting each piece back into its slot shows how the parts make up the whole cell.NGSS crosscutting concept: Structure and Function

Where things stand
The kit is printed and the worksheet generator works. Before any student uses them, a science teacher will check every question. Neither has been tried in a classroom yet.
