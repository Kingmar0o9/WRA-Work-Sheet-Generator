## Scope

Home page of wrastem.com (`index.html`). Mode: Persuade. Audience: middle school science teachers. Action: open the worksheet generator; secondary: explore the real kit model.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The real printed kit is the hero: Mark's own 12-piece plant cell, live in 3D, assembles, comes apart on scroll, then sits beside the resources to be clicked. Refuses the edu template of headline + stock illustration + three equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Seed-packet print grammar, made modern: flat spot inks with no gradients (deep leaf field #124A2B, sunflower, plum, coral, sky) on cool paper stock #F6F7F2, deep green-black ink. Bricolage Grotesque, heavy and tight for display. Ruled spec grid like the back of a packet.
- Amended after the first build: the field moved from #1F7A3E to deep leaf #124A2B because the green chloroplast vanished on the lighter green; the model keeps its own teaching palette (one distinct colour per part, so parts are told apart at a glance), which is a deliberate exception to the six-ink limit for the model only. Chrome stays on the spot inks.
- Raise (one-bit desktop): strict ink limit, every colour is one of six spot inks.
- Raise (split-flap board): the rundown is a fixed-cell ruled grid; values sit in cells, never floating.
- Raise (moon-shadow bazaar): every resource carries its own status ticket (Live / Coming soon).
- Raise (city-pop sleeve): one continuous horizon rule ties the sections together.
- Raise (Massin stage page): size carries volume; the hero line is the loudest thing on the page.

STORY: Teacher sees the real kit, understands it is a hands-on plant cell for grades 6–7, sees it come apart into the five NGSS parts, learns why Mark built it and exactly what the generator gives them and its honest status, then opens the generator or clicks parts to learn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed leaf-green field. Header: wordmark left, three links, "Make a worksheet" button right. Assembled model large, centre-right, tilted slightly left. Headline bottom-left at display scale, one-line subcopy, primary "Make a worksheet" + secondary "See it come apart". Scroll explodes the model in place (sticky stage) with five NGSS labels.

FORM: User-pinned experience (assembled tilt → exploded on scroll → resources left / clickable model right); world from roll candidate 5 of 7 (seed packet). seed:63a4d44616953bc7

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Static GitHub Pages. Fast on school Chromebooks: posters first, 3D only on capable devices, render on demand, one WebGL context. No invented claims; status honest (not yet tested with students). "Why I built it" text is a draft for Mark to rewrite. Non-NGSS part names (golgi, vacuole, vesicle, second mitochondrion) are inferred and need Mark's confirmation.
