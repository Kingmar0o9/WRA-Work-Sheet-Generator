Your science teaching assistant.

Standards AI, for use with the Next Generation Science Standards*

Ask what something is, how to explain it to your students, or for help planning a lesson. It answers in plain words first, then gives the facts. If an answer doesn’t click, say so and it will try another way. Under each answer, “Find it in the NGSS” opens the exact spot in the standard.

Science explanations come from a set of checked science notes. Anything it says about the NGSS is checked against the standard’s text. Answers can still be wrong. About
