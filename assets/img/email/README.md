# Email pictures

Loaded by the thank-you email a teacher gets after sending the Request kits form
(`worker/src/thank-you.js`), from `https://wrastem.com/assets/img/email/`. They are PNG because
email apps can't show WebP. Keep the names: emails already sent point at them.

- `logo.png`: the green logo badge (from `assets/brand/wra-stem-kits-logo-on-green.png`), 440x92.
  It reads on both a white and a black (dark mode) email.
- `kit.png`: the assembled kit (from `assets/img/cell-assembled.webp`), 720 wide, transparent.
