Privacy Policy

What wrastem.com collects, why it is collected, who it is shared with and how long it is kept.

The short version

  • When you send the Request kits form, your answers (including your email address) are collected, along with the IP address the form was sent from and the rough area that address is in.

  • Nothing is sold, and there are no ads, cookies or tracking tools on this site.
  • The server’s logs are kept for up to 7 days. The request email is kept for as long as the request or order needs it.

1. Who this policy covers

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how WRA STEM Kits, a student Compass project at Western Reserve Academy (“WRA STEM Kits”, “we”, “us” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, retains and otherwise processes information, including personal information, in connection with the website located at wrastem.com and its subdomains, including ai.wrastem.com (together, the “Site”), and the features made available through it, including the Request kits form, Standards AI and the Plant Cell Worksheet Generator (together, the “Services”).

In this Policy, “personal information” means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular person. “You” and “your” refer to any person who visits the Site or uses the Services.

2. Your agreement

By ticking the box labelled “I agree to the Privacy Policy” and submitting the Request kits form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and you consent to the collection, use, disclosure and retention of your information as described in it. The Request kits form cannot be submitted, and a request will not be accepted by our server, unless that box has been ticked.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not submit the Request kits form. You may still view the Site and use the Plant Cell Worksheet Generator without submitting any form.

3. Information collected

3.1 Information you provide in the Request kits form

When you submit the Request kits form, we collect the information you enter into it, namely: the number of sets requested; your name, if you choose to give it; the name of your school; your email address; and the contents of any message you write.

3.2 Information collected automatically when you submit the Request kits form

At the time a request is submitted, our server automatically receives and records certain technical information associated with that submission, namely:

  • the Internet Protocol (“IP”) address from which the request was sent;
  • an approximate geographic location inferred from that IP address by our infrastructure provider, which may include a city, state or region, country, postal code, time zone and approximate map coordinates;
  • the name and number of the network operator to which that IP address is assigned (for example, a home internet provider, a mobile carrier, a school district or a data center); and
  • the date and time of the submission.

Location inferred from an IP address is an estimate at roughly the level of a city or town. It is not a street address, it is not derived from your device’s location services, and it may be inaccurate.

3.3 Information derived from your email address

We examine the domain portion of the email address you provide (the part after the “@” sign) in order to determine whether it is associated with a school or other educational institution, whether it belongs to a general-purpose or temporary email provider, whether it corresponds to the school name you entered, and whether it is configured to receive email. To make that last determination, the domain alone, and not your full email address, is submitted as a query to a public Domain Name System (“DNS”) resolver operated by Cloudflare, Inc.

3.4 Information collected when you use Standards AI

When you ask Standards AI a question that requires a written answer, the text of your question and up to the six most recent turns of that conversation are transmitted to our server and to the artificial intelligence model that composes the answer. The question, together with technical details of the request, is recorded in the server’s logs. Your IP address is used to limit how many questions and requests may be sent within a given period. Please do not enter names or other personal information into Standards AI.

3.5 Information collected by our hosting providers

The Site’s pages are hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) and our server is operated on the network of Cloudflare, Inc. As with nearly all websites, these providers receive and may log technical information each time a page or resource is requested, including the IP address, the date and time, the address requested, and browser and device information. That information is processed by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

3.6 Information we do not collect

We do not collect payment card or other financial account information through the Site. We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking technologies. The Plant Cell Worksheet Generator operates entirely within your browser, and the worksheets you create with it are not transmitted to us.

4. How the information is used

We use the information described in Section 3 for the following purposes:

  • to receive, review, respond to and fulfil your request for kits, including by communicating with you at the email address you provide;
  • to verify the authenticity and accuracy of a request and of any resulting order, at the time it is made and at any later time, including by confirming that the request originates from, or on behalf of, a genuine school or educator;
  • to detect, investigate, prevent and respond to spam, fraudulent, deceptive, abusive or automated submissions, and to enforce limits on the number of requests that may be sent;
  • to maintain records of requests and orders, including the fact that you agreed to this Policy;
  • to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve the Site and the Services, including to compose answers in Standards AI; and
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use it to make decisions about you by automated means alone. No request is rejected automatically on the basis of the checks described in Section 5.

5. Checking that a request is real

Because kits are made by hand in limited numbers, each request is reviewed before it is filled. To assist that review, the notification we receive for each request includes the information described in Sections 3.2 and 3.3 together with an automatically generated indication of whether the request appears to come from a school. That indication is based on the characteristics of the email domain and of the network from which the request was sent. It is advisory only, it is reviewed by a person, and it may be wrong. We may retain and refer to this information later in order to verify the truthfulness of a request or order, to resolve a question or dispute about it, or to identify repeated or fraudulent requests.

6. Who the information is shared with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes. We disclose information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Information is processed on our behalf by Cloudflare, Inc., which operates our server, composes Standards AI answers through its Workers AI service, answers DNS queries and delivers the notification email for each request; by GitHub, Inc., which hosts the Site’s pages; and by the provider of the email inbox in which request notifications are received.
  • Project advisers. Information about a request may be disclosed to Western Reserve Academy faculty or staff who supervise this student project, where reasonably necessary to fulfil, verify or answer a question about that request.
  • Map services. The approximate coordinates described in Section 3.2 may be viewed by us on a third-party map service (OpenStreetMap), in which case those coordinates, but not your name, school or email address, are transmitted to that service.
  • Legal and safety reasons. Information may be disclosed where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of any person, or to investigate suspected fraud or abuse.

7. How long it is kept

  • Server logs, which contain a copy of each request and of each Standards AI question together with related technical information, are retained for up to seven (7) days and are then deleted automatically by our infrastructure provider.
  • Request notifications received by email, including the information described in Sections 3.1 to 3.3, are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and fulfil the request, to maintain a record of the request and any resulting order, and to verify it as described in Section 5, after which they are deleted.
  • Saved Standards AI answers. To avoid composing the same answer twice, an answer to a question may be stored for up to seven (7) days and reused when the same question is asked again. A stored answer is not associated with the person who asked the question.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The Site does not set cookies. It stores two settings in your browser’s local storage so that your preferences are remembered on your device: whether the 3D model on the home page is switched on, and whether written answers in Standards AI are switched on. These settings remain on your device, are not transmitted to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encryption of information in transit (HTTPS), restricting our server so that it accepts requests only from the Site’s own pages, limiting the size and number of requests, and limiting access to request notifications. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights

Submitting the Request kits form is voluntary. If you choose not to provide the information marked as required, or do not agree to this Policy, we will not be able to receive your request through the form.

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may withdraw your consent to our continued use of it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, please contact us as described in Section 14. We may need to confirm that a request comes from the person to whom the information relates before acting on it, and we may retain information where we are required or permitted by law to do so.

11. Children

The Site and the Services are intended for teachers and other adults. They are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Site, please contact us so that it can be deleted.

12. Where information is processed

WRA STEM Kits is based in the United States. Information is processed in the United States and in any other country in which our service providers operate facilities. By using the Services, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in a country whose data protection laws differ from those of the place where you live.

13. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time. The revised Policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and it will apply to information collected on or after that date. Requests submitted before a revision remain subject to the version of this Policy in effect when they were submitted, unless you agree otherwise.

14. Contact

Questions about this Policy, and requests concerning your personal information, may be sent using the message box on the Request kits form, or by replying to any email you have received from us about your request.
